# Partner Center Evidence Rules

Recheck current Partner Center and Microsoft policy before final entry.

## Keywords

For MSIX listings, use at most **7** Keywords. Each Keyword may have up to **40 characters**. The full set may contain no more than **21 separate words**. Keep every Keyword relevant. Exclude pricing language and other product titles unless the publisher also publishes that product.

## Screenshots

At least one screenshot is required; four or more is a strong internal standard for a desktop listing. Desktop screenshots must be PNG files at least **1366×768** and no larger than **50 MB**. Keep critical content in the top two-thirds. Do not add logos, marketing messages, captions, arrows, browser chrome, device frames, or fabricated results to screenshots. Captions, if used, must be factual and 200 characters or fewer.

## Accurate representation

Names, descriptions, screenshots, category, and Keywords must accurately describe the actual product and its relationship to third parties. The final package, listing claims, screenshots, and test record must describe the same candidate.
